# Magic Moment and Aha Activation

## The "aha" moment

**The first time the bank does something the customer couldn't do themselves** — instantly, clearly, securely:

- A salary lands and is **automatically sorted** into pots before the customer thinks about it.
- A fraudulent transaction is **flagged and frozen in one tap**, with the card usable again in seconds.
- The assistant answers **"where is my transfer?"** in one sentence, with the status and ETA.
- A savings goal reaches **100%** with a satisfying, honest celebration.

## Activation funnel

| Step | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up | Registration started | — |
| Verify identity | KYC completed | <10 min |
| First deposit | Account funded | Day 1 |
| First payment | Transfer/bill paid | Week 1 |
| First "aha" | Auto-sort / instant freeze / assistant resolution | Week 1–2 |
| Habit formed | 3+ active weeks/month | Month 1 |

## Activation design

- **Time-to-value:** first value in <10 minutes (onboarding) and <24 hours (first useful action).
- **Guided setup:** progressive disclosure; don't front-load 485 features.
- **Proactive value:** the assistant surfaces a useful insight or completes a task unprompted (e.g., "you could save $40/mo by switching this subscription").
- **Trust cues:** visible security, plain language, human escalation when needed.
