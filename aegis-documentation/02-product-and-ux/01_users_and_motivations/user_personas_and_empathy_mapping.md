# User Personas and Empathy Mapping

## Primary personas

### Persona 1 — "Everyday Emma" (Retail)
- **Demographics:** 28, urban, salaried, mobile-first.
- **Goals:** get paid, spend, save, split bills, build credit.
- **Frustrations:** hidden fees, slow support, complex apps.
- **Quote:** "I just want to see my money clearly and not get ripped off on fees."

### Persona 2 — "Savvy Sam" (Self-directed investor)
- **Goals:** trade, track portfolio, minimize fees.
- **Frustrations:** high brokerage fees, poor analytics.
- **Quote:** "Show me my real performance, not marketing."

### Persona 3 — "Business Bea" (SME owner)
- **Goals:** get paid, manage expenses, stay tax-compliant.
- **Frustrations:** admin overload, disconnected tools.
- **Quote:** "I didn't start a business to do bookkeeping."

### Persona 4 — "Compliance Chris" (Analyst)
- **Goals:** investigate alerts fast, file defensible SARs.
- **Frustrations:** 90%+ false positives, manual research.
- **Quote:** "I spend 70% of my time gathering evidence, 5% analysing."

### Persona 5 — "Access Alex" (Accessibility)
- **Goals:** complete any journey independently.
- **Frustrations:** inaccessible apps, poor screen-reader support.
- **Quote:** "If I can't use it, I can't bank."

## Empathy mapping (Everyday Emma)

| Says | Thinks | Does | Feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Where's my transfer?" | "Is it stuck?" | Checks app repeatedly | Anxious |
| "Why was I declined?" | "Am I being judged?" | Contacts support | Frustrated |
| "I want to save." | "I never have enough." | Tries budgets | Guilt |

## Design implications

- **Clarity first** — show money plainly, explain declines, confirm actions.
- **Speed** — instant feedback on every action.
- **Trust** — visible security (passkeys, instant freeze), plain language.
- **Inclusion** — WCAG 2.2 AA is a first-class requirement, not a retrofit.
