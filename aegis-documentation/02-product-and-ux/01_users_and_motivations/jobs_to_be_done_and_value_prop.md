# Jobs to Be Done and Value Proposition

## Core jobs-to-be-done

| When… | I want to… | So I can… | Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| When I'm paid | Sort my salary automatically | Save and budget without effort | Salary sorting, pots |
| When I see something I want | Pay instantly / BNPL | Buy now, manage cash flow | Payments, BNPL |
| When I suspect fraud | Freeze my card in one tap | Protect my money | Card controls |
| When I need help | Get an answer 24/7 | Resolve without waiting | Agentic assistant |
| When I want to grow | Save toward a goal | Reach milestones | Savings goals, round-ups |
| When I need credit | Get a fast, fair decision | Access funds | Instant lending |
| When I travel | Spend without FX fees | Save on conversion | Multi-currency, zero-FX card |
| When I run a business | Get paid and stay compliant | Focus on the business | Business account, invoicing, MTD |

## Value proposition

**"The bank that thinks with you."** Incumbent-grade trust + neobank-grade UX + best-in-class security (passkeys, real-time fraud, scam detection) + agentic AI that escalates to humans.

## Differentiation vs 2026 market

- Security whitespace (only 5% have passkeys, 12% in-app fraud detection).
- Agentic assistant that actually completes tasks and hands off cleanly.
- Full accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) as a legal + ethical advantage.
- Explainable AI (every decision traceable).
