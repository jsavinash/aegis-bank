# Jobs to Be Done and Value Proposition

## Core jobs-to-be-done

| When… | I want to… | So I can… | Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| I'm paid | Sort my salary automatically | Save and budget without effort | Salary sorting, pots, auto-save rules |
| I see something I want | Pay instantly / use BNPL | Buy now, manage cash flow | Payments, BNPL, instant transfers |
| I suspect fraud | Freeze my card in one tap | Protect my money | Card controls, one-tap freeze |
| I need help | Get an answer 24/7 with human escalation for fraud/disputes | Resolve without waiting | Agentic assistant (voice/chat) + live chat |
| I want to grow | Save toward a goal, reach milestones | Build wealth automatically | Savings goals, round-ups, salary sorting |
| I need credit | Get a fast, fair decision with explainability | Access funds | Instant personal loan, underwriting copilot |
| I travel | Spend without FX fees, with global QR | Save on conversion | Multi-currency wallets, zero-FX conversion, global QR |
| I run a business | Get paid and stay compliant (MTD) | Focus on the business | Business accounts, invoicing, payroll, tax compliance |
| I need accessible support | Complete every journey with screen-reader & motor/cognitive support | Inclusive access | WCAG 2.2 AA, accessibility support adjustments |

## Value proposition

**"The bank that thinks with you."** Incumbent-grade trust + neobank-grade UX + best-in-class security (passkeys, real-time fraud, scam detection, card controls) + agentic AI that **escalates to humans, never deflects** (J.D. Power: satisfaction drops when fraud/disputes force users into self-service loops). Full accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA as legal + ethical advantage); explainable AI (every decision traceable with citation-required grounding).

## Differentiation vs 2026 market

- Security whitespace (only ~5% of apps ship passkeys; only ~17% have in-app scam warnings — Ostorlab 2026).
- Agentic assistant that **completes tasks and hands off cleanly** to a human for fraud/disputes — not a self-service trap.
- Full WCAG 2.2 AA compliance as a legal advantage (EU EAA) and ethical differentiator.
- AI decisions are **grounded, guarded, eval-gated, and auditable** — no hallucinated risk scores, no opaque narratives.
