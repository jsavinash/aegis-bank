# 07 — Personas and Business Scenarios

## Personas

| Persona | Goals | Pain points | Key scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Everyday Retail** | Get paid, spend, pay, send, budget | Hidden fees, slow support | Pay a bill, split with a friend, freeze a card |
| **Saver / Goal-setter** | Automate savings, earn interest | Low rates, no automation | Create a goal, round-up save, salary sort |
| **Borrower** | Personal loan / BNPL / overdraft | Slow decisions, opaque pricing | Apply for a loan, use BNPL at checkout |
| **Self-directed Investor** | Trade, track portfolio | High fees, poor tools | Buy fractional shares, view portfolio health |
| **Hands-off Investor** | Robo advice, auto-rebalance | Advice reserved for the wealthy | Onboard robo, auto-rebalance |
| **Small Business** | Business account, invoicing, tax | Admin overload | Send invoice, reconcile, file tax |
| **Parent / Teen** | Kids' cards, allowances, controls | No safe way to give kids cards | Set allowance, freeze teen card |
| **Traveller / Expat** | Multi-currency, low FX, global QR | FX markups | Hold USD/EUR, pay by QR abroad |
| **Vulnerable / Accessibility** | Accessible, supportive service | Inaccessible apps | Complete a journey with a screen reader |
| **Compliance Analyst** | Investigate alerts, file SARs | Alert overload, manual research | Investigate an alert, draft a SAR |
| **Fraud Analyst** | Triage alerts, stop fraud | Too many false positives | Review a triaged alert, freeze a card |

## Business scenarios

1. **Onboard** a new customer end-to-end in <10 minutes (digital KYC).
2. **Detect & stop** a card-testing fraud ring in real time.
3. **Investigate** an AML alert and file a SAR with a human sign-off.
4. **Answer** a customer's "where is my transfer?" via the agentic assistant.
5. **Approve** a straightforward personal loan straight-through; escalate complex ones.
