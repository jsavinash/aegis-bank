# Executive Summary

## What we are building

**Aegis Bank** is an AI-first retail digital bank that combines incumbent-grade trust with neobank-grade UX and best-in-class security. It offers a full retail banking surface (~485 features: accounts, cards, payments, lending, wealth, business, open banking, compliance) delivered through mobile, web, voice, and an agentic assistant.

## The thesis

The **AI layer is the product**, not a feature. Aegis uses AI as the fraud engine, the AML investigator, the compliance analyst, the virtual assistant, and the credit underwriter. This is why the program is structured "AI-first": AI is a first-class layer in the architecture, the data model, the delivery pipeline, and the operating model.

## Why now (2026 market)

- **Security whitespace:** only 5% of banking apps ship passkeys; 12% ship in-app fraud detection; 17% a scam warning (Ostorlab 2,686-app scan). Aegis makes these table-stakes.
- **AI maturity:** agentic AI rose from 15% to 39% of new functional AI use cases in Q3 2026 (Evident). JPMorgan's LLM Suite serves 250k employees; agentic AML is shipping at Flagright, Marble, Refute, RiskFront.
- **Regulation forces quality:** EU AI Act, PSD2/PSD3, EAA, GDPR, AML/KYC — all reward explainable, auditable, human-accountable AI, which is exactly Aegis's design.

## What success looks like

- 99.95%+ availability; <150ms fraud decisions; WCAG 2.2 AA; PCI-DSS/SOC 2/ISO 27001.
- Fraud false positives cut 40–60%; AML analyst time per alert down ~70%; contact deflection 25–40%.
- **North-star:** monthly active customers completing at least one high-value digital journey (payment, application, savings goal).
- A defensible, audit-ready AI estate where every decision is traceable to data, model, policy, and human.

## Investment posture

Buy the core (Thought Machine/Mambu) rather than build; build the differentiated AI/risk plane; buy commodity data (KYC/AML/fraud feeds). Sequence 2–3 deep wins (fraud, servicing, AML) before breadth, and Ship a production-grade Phase 1 release (accounts, cards, payments, security base) in ~3 months.
