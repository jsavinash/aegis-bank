# Data Privacy and Retention

## Principles

- **Minimisation** — collect only what's needed.
- **Purpose limitation** — use only for stated purposes.
- **Transparency** — customers see data access.
- **Erasure** — honour GDPR/CCPA DSARs.

## Retention schedule

| Data | Retention | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Transaction records** | 7 years | AML / tax |
| **KYC / CDD records** | 5 years after relationship ends | AML |
| **SARs / STRs** | 5 years | AML |
| **Audit logs** | 7 years | Regulatory |
| **Marketing consent** | Until withdrawn | GDPR |
| **Closed account data** | Per policy + legal hold | Contract / law |

## Data subject rights

| Right | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Access** | Self-service export |
| **Rectification** | Update profile |
| **Erasure** | DSAR workflow (+ crypto-shredding) |
| **Portability** | Machine-readable export |
| **Objection** | Opt-out (marketing) |

## Privacy by design

- **Data protection impact assessments (DPIA)** for high-risk processing.
- **Consent** for AI training on customer data.
- **Data residency** — region-locked processing/storage.
- **Encryption** at rest and in transit.

## Breach response

- Detect → contain → assess → notify (regulator + customers per GDPR timelines) → remediate.
